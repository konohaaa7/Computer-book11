Introduction of Computer

A computer is an advanced electronic device designed to accept raw data as input. It processes this data with high speed and precision using a specific set of instructions called a program. Once processed, it generates accurate output results and stores them for future use.Modern computers consist of two main components: hardware and software. The physical parts you can touch are hardware, while the internal programs are software. Because of their incredible speed, vast storage capacity, and versatility, computers are now vital tools used globally in education, business, healthcare, and daily communication.
